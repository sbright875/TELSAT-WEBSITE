Telecom Security

Equipment Identity Register

Handset theft is a persistent problem on GSM networks: a stolen phone still works the moment a thief drops in a legitimate SIM, since the network has no way to tell the handset itself apart from a genuine one. Telsat's EIR closes that gap by identifying the physical device — independent of subscriber, SIM or number — and checking it against operator-defined access rules before it ever registers.

When a handset attempts to register on a new MSC or VLR, its IMEI is checked in real time against Whitelist, Greylist and Blacklist records, with optional IMEI–IMSI correlation for finer control. Disallowed or stolen devices are blocked from registering, while flagged devices can still be tracked.

  • Real-time IMEI checks against Whitelist, Greylist and Blacklist, across 2G/3G/4G/LTE
  • Scales to 100M+ IMEI records and tens of thousands of transactions per second
  • Works across Diameter, SS7, HTTP/2 and other network protocols
  • IMEI–IMSI correlation and IMSI-range logic for granular access control
  • Independent, centralized, standards-compliant (ETSI, 3GPP, GSMA) design
  • Carrier-grade, geo-redundant and fully GUI-based — no scripting required
