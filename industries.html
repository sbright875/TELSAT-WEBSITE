Public services

Public Sector / Government

Public institutions operate under budget constraints, regulatory obligations, high security requirements and growing expectations for convenient citizen services. Telsat delivers secure cloud infrastructure, custom applications, collaboration systems, data platforms and digital transformation programmes designed for resilient public operations.

Our teams combine public-sector awareness with engineering depth to streamline service delivery, strengthen data protection and improve citizen engagement.

Critical infrastructure

Energy & Utilities

We support power generation, distribution companies, renewable-energy providers and utility operators with smart-grid connectivity, energy-management systems, asset monitoring, smart metering, secure data platforms and operational technology integration.

Solutions are aligned to operational efficiency, reliability, sustainability and the security of critical infrastructure.

High-risk operations

Oil, Gas & Mining

Exploration, drilling, refinery and mining environments demand safe, resilient and highly available technology. We provide industrial connectivity, IoT-enabled monitoring, data-management systems, cybersecurity, safety platforms and remote operational support.

Our approach helps operators improve visibility, mitigate risk, increase efficiency and reduce avoidable downtime.

Digital health

Healthcare

Hospitals, clinics and healthcare providers need dependable systems that support clinical workflows while protecting sensitive information. Telsat delivers electronic health platforms, telehealth infrastructure, secure networking, patient-management tools, analytics and managed support.

We design around clinical continuity, privacy, regulatory compliance and better patient outcomes.

Regulated finance

Banking, Financial Services & Insurance

Banks, insurers, credit institutions and investment firms require secure, compliant and always-available technology. We support core platforms, secure payment environments, risk-management systems, identity controls, cloud transformation and cybersecurity operations.

Our solutions strengthen resilience, improve customer experience and help regulated organisations modernise with confidence.

Digital finance

Fintech

Fintech platforms live and die by uptime, latency and data integrity. We support payment gateways, mobile money integrations, trading and market-data systems, API infrastructure and the secure, high-availability networks these platforms depend on.

Our engineering approach helps fintech teams scale confidently while meeting the security and compliance expectations of regulators, partners and customers.

Connected learning

Education

We help schools, universities and training institutions create secure, connected learning environments through campus networks, learning-management systems, smart classrooms, collaboration tools, student-information platforms and cloud services.

Technology is aligned with curriculum, administration and teaching goals to improve access, engagement and institutional efficiency.

Industry 4.0

Manufacturing

Manufacturers need integrated, reliable systems from the factory floor to the enterprise. Telsat supports industrial networking, process automation, data analytics, asset tracking, cybersecurity, cloud platforms and business applications.

Our solutions help improve productivity, quality, visibility and cost control across small-scale facilities and large industrial operations.

Carrier-grade engineering

Telecommunications

We serve mobile operators, internet service providers and communications companies with core-network integration, signalling platforms, network management, customer-experience systems, billing and revenue solutions, cloud infrastructure and cybersecurity.

Telsat combines telecom domain knowledge with software and infrastructure engineering to address complex operational and commercial requirements.

Enterprise growth

Private enterprises need practical technology that drives growth, improves productivity and supports customer experience. We deliver tailored infrastructure, cloud, cybersecurity, custom software, managed services and digital workplace solutions across diverse industries.
