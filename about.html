About Telsat Engineering Services

Engineering connected, resilient and sustainable infrastructure for Africa.

Founded in 2013, Telsat Engineering Services Ltd. stands at the forefront of technological innovation, transforming Electrical Engineering, Power Systems, Information Technology, Data and Telecommunications Infrastructure across Uganda and the African continent. Our mission is to deliver integrated, sustainable solutions that empower communities, businesses and governments to thrive in a rapidly evolving digital world.

With a team of seasoned experts, Telsat provides comprehensive services—from strategic consulting and advanced design to implementation, maintenance and capacity building. Our commitment to quality, safety and client satisfaction has helped us build trusted relationships across the public and private sectors.

Our Vision

To be a leading provider of innovative IT, telecommunications, electrical and power engineering solutions in Africa, driving sustainable development and enhancing connectivity for communities across the continent.

Our Mission

To empower our clients through exceptional, value-driven services in electrical engineering, information technology, power and telecommunications. We use innovative solutions to accelerate digital transformation, strengthen cybersecurity and optimise infrastructure for long-term growth and resilience.
